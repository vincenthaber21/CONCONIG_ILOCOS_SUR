Tips

Term (months)
How many monthly payments the loan is split into. Term does not change interest — only the installment count.
Interest rate
Type the formula rate, such as 0.180. New applications start with this rate. You can still change it on a single loan.
Usable days
Enable on this product for usable-days loans. Formula: interest = principal × rate × (days ÷ 360); amount pay = interest + partial pay. Example: 10,000 × 0.18 × (16 ÷ 360) = ₱80.
